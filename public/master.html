Questions

Is the score the same as a real company’s ATS?

No. Every company configures its own system. The score estimates how well your resume matches the posting and how safely it parses.

Why is my score low with no job description?

Without a posting, matching uses the role’s typical skills. Paste a real job description for a sharper result.

Does Deep AI invent achievements?

It is instructed to use only facts in your resume, uses placeholders for missing numbers, and reports a fabrication risk. Always review before sending.

Can I upload a Word file?

Yes. .docx files are read in your browser. Save old .doc files as .docx or PDF first.
